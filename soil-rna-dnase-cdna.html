Decision ~5 min

Use routine TURBO DNA-free treatment. At the current concentration estimate, the current input is approximately 500 ng total RNA, which is far below the high-nucleic-acid range that would push this toward a rigorous DNase treatment.

Estimated total time: about 1.5-2.5 hours of active hands-on work, or about 2.5-4 hours elapsed if you include incubation, centrifugation, optional RNA quantification, and cDNA synthesis. If you only do DNase treatment and storage, plan about 45-75 minutes elapsed.

After DNase treatment, quantify the RNA if possible. Use only an aliquot for cDNA synthesis and keep the rest of the DNase-treated RNA frozen.

cDNA Input Planner ~2-5 min

DNase treatment and cDNA input have different limits. You may DNase-treat the full RNA sample, such as 50 uL, then use only an aliquot for cDNA. The SuperScript IV limits apply to the cDNA reaction: up to 11 uL RNA in the RNA-primer mix and 10 pg to 5 ug total RNA per 20 uL RT reaction.

Use post-DNase RNA concentration if measured. If not measured yet, use the starting estimate.

Part A: TURBO DNA-free Treatment ~45-75 min elapsed; ~20-30 min active

Component Volume per sample
Soil RNA sample 50 uL
10X TURBO DNase Buffer 5 uL
TURBO DNase enzyme 1 uL
DNase digestion volume 56 uL
  1. Thaw RNA on ice.
  2. Gently mix RNA by flicking or pipetting. Briefly spin down.
  3. Combine 50 uL RNA, 5 uL 10X TURBO DNase Buffer, and 1 uL TURBO DNase enzyme in an RNase-free tube.
  4. Mix gently by pipetting. Briefly spin down.
  5. Incubate at 37 C for 20-30 min.
  6. During incubation, fully resuspend the DNase Inactivation Reagent until it is an even slurry.
  7. Add 5.6 uL resuspended DNase Inactivation Reagent to each sample.
  8. Mix well so the inactivation reagent is suspended throughout the sample.
  9. Incubate 5 min at room temperature, flicking 2-3 times during the incubation.
  10. Centrifuge at 10,000 x g for 1.5 min.
  11. Transfer the clear supernatant to a fresh RNase-free tube without disturbing the pellet.

Expected recovered volume is less than the full reaction volume because some liquid should be left behind above the pellet.

Part B: Post-DNase QC and Storage ~15-60 min, depending on QC

  1. Label the recovered tube as sampleID_DNase-treated_RNA_date.
  2. Keep on ice for same-day cDNA synthesis.
  3. If not continuing the same day, store at -80 C if available. Use -20 C only as a short-term fallback.
  4. Avoid repeated freeze-thaw cycles.

Recommended Checks

  • Re-quantify RNA with Qubit RNA HS/BR, NanoDrop, Bioanalyzer, TapeStation, or the lab's available RNA method.
  • Check residual DNA if a dsDNA assay is available.
  • Include a no-RT control during cDNA/qPCR work to detect residual genomic DNA.

Part C: SuperScript IV cDNA Synthesis ~60-90 min elapsed; ~25-40 min active

For soil microbial or mixed environmental RNA, use random hexamers unless the target assay specifically requires oligo(dT) or a gene-specific primer.

RNA-Primer Mix

Component Volume
Random hexamers, 50 ng/uL 1 uL
10 mM dNTP mix 1 uL
DNase-treated RNA 10 uL
DEPC-treated water 1 uL
Total 13 uL
  1. Add primer, dNTPs, RNA, and water to a PCR tube on ice.
  2. Mix gently and briefly centrifuge.
  3. Heat at 65 C for 5 min.
  4. Immediately place on ice for at least 1 min.
  5. Briefly centrifuge and return to ice.

RT Reaction Mix

Component Volume
5X SSIV Buffer 4 uL
100 mM DTT 1 uL
Ribonuclease Inhibitor 1 uL
SuperScript IV Reverse Transcriptase 1 uL
Total RT mix 7 uL
  1. Thaw and mix the 5X SSIV Buffer according to the kit manual.
  2. Prepare the RT reaction mix on ice.
  3. Add 7 uL RT reaction mix to the 13 uL annealed RNA-primer mix.
  4. Mix gently and briefly centrifuge.
  5. For a no-RT control, replace 1 uL SuperScript IV Reverse Transcriptase with 1 uL DEPC-treated water.

Optional RNA removal: add 1 uL E. coli RNase H and incubate at 37 C for 20 min.

Store cDNA at -20 C, or use immediately for PCR/qPCR. As a starting point, use cDNA as no more than about 10% of the final downstream reaction volume.

Stop Points check before continuing

  • Stop and ask before using the whole DNase-treated RNA sample for cDNA.
  • Stop and ask if the recovered RNA concentration is much lower than expected.
  • Stop and ask if the no-RT control amplifies strongly.
  • Stop and ask if the downstream facility wants RNA, not cDNA.
