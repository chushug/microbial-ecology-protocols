# Soil DNA Extraction - DNeasy PowerLyzer PowerSoil

Bench-ready protocol for extracting microbial DNA from up to 0.25 g soil
with the QIAGEN DNeasy PowerLyzer PowerSoil Kit.

**Format**Individual spin columns

**Input**Up to 0.25 g soil

**Elution**100 uL

**Total elapsed**~30 min

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Before Starting

**Prepare the kit**

- Perform every centrifugation step at room temperature (15-25 deg C).
- If Solution C1 contains precipitate, heat it at 60 deg C until the
  precipitate dissolves.
- Shake Solution C4 thoroughly before use.
- Ensure that PowerBead Tubes rotate freely in the centrifuge; do not
  exceed 10,000 x g.

**Chemical safety**

Solution C5 contains ethanol and is flammable. PowerBead Solution and
Solution C4 contain guanidine salts. Do not add bleach or acidic
solutions directly to waste containing these reagents.

**Choose the lysis setting**

The vendor’s starting setting is 4,000 rpm for 45 s for clay soils and
2,500 rpm for 45 s for loose, granular, or high-organic soils on a
PowerLyzer 24. Record the setting used for each extraction batch.

## Part A: Lysis and Clarification

*~10-15 min*

Add up to **0.25 g soil** to a PowerBead Tube.

Add **750 uL PowerBead Solution** and **60 uL Solution C1**. Invert
several times or vortex briefly.

Homogenize using the validated setting for the soil. For a PowerLyzer
24, use **4,000 rpm for 45 s** for clay soils or **2,500 rpm for 45 s**
for loose, granular, or high-organic soils. Alternatively, secure tubes
horizontally in the 24-place Vortex Adapter and vortex at maximum speed
for **10 min**; for more than 12 preps, extend vortexing by 5-10 min.

Centrifuge at room temperature for **30 s at 10,000 x g**. For clay
soils or an incompletely pelleted sample, centrifuge for **3 min at
10,000 x g**.

Transfer the supernatant to a clean 2 mL Collection Tube. Expect
approximately **400-500 uL**; some soil particles may remain.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Part B: Inhibitor Removal and DNA Binding

*~15-20 min*

Add **250 uL Solution C2**, vortex for **5 s**, and incubate at **2-8
deg C for 5 min**.

Centrifuge for **1 min at 10,000 x g**. Without disturbing the pellet,
transfer up to **600 uL supernatant** to a clean 2 mL Collection Tube.

Add **200 uL Solution C3**, vortex briefly, and incubate at **2-8 deg C
for 5 min**.

Centrifuge for **1 min at 10,000 x g**. Without disturbing the pellet,
transfer up to **750 uL supernatant** to a clean 2 mL Collection Tube.

Add **1,200 uL Solution C4** and vortex for **5 s**.

Load **675 uL** onto an MB Spin Column. Centrifuge for **1 min at 10,000
x g** and discard the flow-through. Repeat with a second 675 uL load,
then load and centrifuge the remaining mixture. Three loads are
required.

**Validated workflow note**

The vendor states that the 5 min incubations after Solution C2 and
Solution C3 may be omitted. Retain them when continuing an extraction
workflow that was previously validated with these incubations.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Part C: Wash and Elute DNA

*~5 min*

Add **500 uL Solution C5** to the MB Spin Column and centrifuge for **30
s at 10,000 x g**.

Discard the flow-through and centrifuge again for **1 min at 10,000 x
g** to remove residual wash solution.

Place the MB Spin Column in a clean 2 mL Collection Tube without
splashing Solution C5 onto the column.

Add **100 uL Solution C6** to the center of the white filter membrane.
Centrifuge for **30 s at 10,000 x g**.

Discard the MB Spin Column. The eluate contains DNA ready for downstream
applications.

**Storage**

Store eluted DNA frozen at -20 to -80 deg C. Solution C6 does not
contain EDTA; avoid repeated freeze-thaw cycles.

## Stop Points

**Stop and ask before:**

- Using a bead-homogenization setting that has not been validated for
  the sample type.
- Continuing if the sample is not pelleted after the specified
  centrifugation step and the needed clarification time is uncertain.
- Disturbing or transferring an inhibitor-removal pellet.
- Skipping the second post-wash centrifugation, because residual ethanol
  from Solution C5 can inhibit downstream DNA applications.
- Processing an extraction batch without complete sample identification
  or the recorded lysis setting.
