# Soil RNA Extraction — RNeasy PowerSoil Total RNA

Bench manual for soil total RNA extraction using the QIAGEN RNeasy PowerSoil Total RNA Kit.

<div class="facts">
  <div class="fact"><strong>Input</strong><span>≤2 g soil</span></div>
  <div class="fact"><strong>Final volume</strong><span>100 µL</span></div>
  <div class="fact"><strong>Hazard</strong><span>Phenol/chloroform</span></div>
  <div class="fact"><strong>Elapsed time</strong><span>~2.5–3.5 h</span></div>
</div>

---

## Before Starting

!!! danger "Chemical hazard — phenol/chloroform/isoamyl alcohol"
    This protocol requires **3.5 mL phenol/chloroform/isoamyl alcohol (pH 6.5–8.0), user-supplied**. Work in a chemical fume hood. Wear appropriate PPE (gloves, lab coat, eye protection). Dispose of phenol/chloroform waste according to your institution's hazardous waste policy.

!!! info "Notes"
    - Perform all centrifugation steps at room temperature (15–25°C).
    - Wear **RNase-free gloves** at all times and decontaminate the work area with RNase removal solution before starting.
    - Store the kit at room temperature (15–25°C) until the expiry date.

---

## Materials

**Kit Solutions**

- PowerBead Solution
- Solution SR1
- Solution IRS
- Solution SR3
- Solution SR4
- Solution SR5
- Solution SR6 (elution)
- Solution SR7 (resuspension)

**User-Supplied**

- Phenol/chloroform/isoamyl alcohol, pH 6.5–8.0, 3.5 mL per sample

**Kit Hardware**

- 15 mL PowerBead Tube
- 15 mL Collection Tubes
- 2.2 mL Collection Tubes
- JetStar Mini Column

**Equipment**

- Vortex mixer with Vortex Adapter (cat. no. 13000-V1-15)
- Centrifuge capable of 2500 × g and 13,000 × g
- Chemical fume hood
- Heat block or water bath at 45°C (if pellet is difficult to resuspend)

---

## Part A: Bead Beating & Phase Separation

*~45–60 min*

<ul class="checklist">
  <li><input type="checkbox"><span>Add up to <strong>2 g soil</strong> to the 15 mL PowerBead Tube.</span></li>
  <li><input type="checkbox"><span>Add <strong>2.5 mL PowerBead Solution</strong>, <strong>0.25 mL Solution SR1</strong>, and <strong>0.8 mL Solution IRS</strong>.</span></li>
  <li><input type="checkbox"><span>Add <strong>3.5 mL phenol/chloroform/isoamyl alcohol</strong> (pH 6.5–8.0) in the fume hood. Cap and vortex until the biphasic layer disappears.</span></li>
  <li><input type="checkbox"><span>Place the PowerBead Tube on the Vortex Adapter and vortex at <strong>maximum speed for 15 min</strong>.</span></li>
  <li><input type="checkbox"><span>Centrifuge at <strong>2500 × g for 10 min</strong>.</span></li>
  <li><input type="checkbox"><span>Transfer the <strong>upper aqueous phase</strong> to a clean 15 mL Collection Tube. Avoid the interphase and lower phenol layer. Discard the phenol/chloroform/isoamyl alcohol as hazardous waste. <em>Note: In high-organic-matter soils, the biphasic layer may be firm and need to be pierced to remove the bottom phenol layer.</em></span></li>
</ul>

---

## Part B: Precipitation & Cleanup

*~55–70 min*

<ul class="checklist">
  <li><input type="checkbox"><span>Add <strong>1.5 mL Solution SR3</strong> to the aqueous phase. Vortex to mix. Incubate at <strong>2–8°C for 10 min</strong>. Centrifuge at <strong>2500 × g for 10 min</strong> at room temperature.</span></li>
  <li><input type="checkbox"><span>Transfer the supernatant (without disturbing the pellet) to a new 15 mL Collection Tube.</span></li>
  <li><input type="checkbox"><span>Add <strong>5 mL Solution SR4</strong>. Invert or vortex to mix. Incubate at room temperature for <strong>30 min</strong>.</span></li>
  <li><input type="checkbox"><span>Centrifuge at <strong>2500 × g for 30 min</strong>.</span></li>
  <li><input type="checkbox"><span>Decant the supernatant. Invert the Collection Tube on a paper towel for <strong>5 min</strong>.</span></li>
  <li><input type="checkbox"><span>Shake Solution SR5 to mix. Add <strong>1 mL Solution SR5</strong> to the tube. Resuspend the pellet completely by pipetting or vortexing. If difficult to resuspend, heat at 45°C for 10 min, then vortex. Repeat until fully dissolved.</span></li>
</ul>

---

## Part C: Column Purification

*~30–40 min*

<ul class="checklist">
  <li><input type="checkbox"><span>Prepare one JetStar Mini Column per sample: remove cap from a 15 mL Collection Tube and place the JetStar Mini Column inside so it hangs in the tube.</span></li>
  <li><input type="checkbox"><span>Add <strong>2 mL Solution SR5</strong> to the JetStar Mini Column. Allow to gravity flow completely. Do not let the column dry out before loading sample.</span></li>
  <li><input type="checkbox"><span>Load the RNA isolation sample (from Part B Step 6) onto the JetStar Mini Column. Allow to gravity flow through.</span></li>
  <li><input type="checkbox"><span>Add <strong>1 mL Solution SR5</strong> to the column. Allow to gravity flow through completely.</span></li>
  <li><input type="checkbox"><span>Transfer the JetStar Mini Column to a <strong>new</strong> 15 mL Collection Tube. Shake Solution SR6 to mix. Add <strong>1 mL Solution SR6</strong> to the column to elute bound RNA. Allow to gravity flow.</span></li>
</ul>

---

## Part D: RNA Precipitation & Resuspension

*~35–50 min*

<ul class="checklist">
  <li><input type="checkbox"><span>Transfer the eluted RNA to a 2.2 mL Collection Tube. Add <strong>1 mL Solution SR4</strong>. Invert at least once to mix. Incubate at <strong>−15°C to −30°C for ≥10 min</strong>.</span></li>
  <li><input type="checkbox"><span>Centrifuge at <strong>13,000 × g for 15 min</strong> to pellet RNA.</span></li>
  <li><input type="checkbox"><span>Decant the supernatant. Invert the 2.2 mL Collection Tube onto a paper towel for <strong>10 min</strong> to air dry the pellet.</span></li>
  <li><input type="checkbox"><span>Resuspend the RNA pellet in <strong>100 µL Solution SR7</strong>. RNA is ready for downstream use or storage.</span></li>
</ul>

!!! tip "Storage"
    Store RNA at −80°C for long-term use. Use −20°C only as a short-term fallback. Avoid repeated freeze-thaw cycles. Quantify with Qubit RNA HS/BR or equivalent before use.

## Stop Points

!!! danger "Stop and ask before:"
    - Continuing if you cannot see a clear phase separation after bead beating (Step A-6).
    - Discarding the pellet in Part B if you are unsure which layer is the pellet.
    - Proceeding to downstream cDNA synthesis if RNA concentration is much lower than expected or A260/A280 < 1.8.
