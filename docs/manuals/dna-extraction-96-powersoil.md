# Soil DNA Extraction — DNeasy 96 PowerSoil Pro

Bench manual for 96-well plate soil DNA extraction using the QIAGEN DNeasy 96 PowerSoil Pro Kit.

<div class="facts">
  <div class="fact"><strong>Format</strong><span>96-well plate</span></div>
  <div class="fact"><strong>Input</strong><span>≤0.25 g soil/well</span></div>
  <div class="fact"><strong>Elution volume</strong><span>100 µL</span></div>
  <div class="fact"><strong>Elapsed time</strong><span>~2–3 h</span></div>
</div>

---

## Before Starting

!!! warning "Storage"
    Solution CD2 must be stored at 2–8°C upon arrival. All other reagents store at room temperature (15–25°C).

!!! info "Notes"
    - If Solution CD3 has precipitate, heat at 60°C until dissolved before use.
    - Use a **96-well plate rotor** for all centrifugation steps; perform at room temperature.
    - Use extra-long pipette tips (1000–1250 µL) for collection microtubes (CMTRs).

---

## Materials

**Named kit**

- DNeasy 96 PowerSoil Pro Kit (QIAGEN)

**Kit Solutions**

- Solution CD1 (bead beating buffer)
- Solution CD2 (inhibitor removal; store 2–8°C)
- Solution CD3 (binding buffer)
- Solution EA (wash 1)
- Solution C5 (wash 2)
- Solution C6 (elution buffer)

**Kit Hardware**

- PowerBead Pro Plate (96-well)
- Square well mat and sealing film
- QIAamp 96 Spin Plate
- S-Block (deep-well block)
- Collection microtubes, racked (CMTRs) with caps

**Equipment**

- TissueLyser II with Adapter Plate Sets (cat. no. 11990) and silicone compression mat
- Centrifuge with 96-well plate rotor, capable of 4500 × g
- Vortex mixer

---

## Part A: Sample Loading & Bead Beating

*~30–40 min*

<ul class="checklist">
  <li><input type="checkbox"><span>Briefly centrifuge the PowerBead Pro Plate to settle the beads at the bottom of each well.</span></li>
  <li><input type="checkbox"><span>Remove and discard the square well mat. Add ≤0.25 g soil (or ≤0.1 g stool) and <strong>800 µL Solution CD1</strong> to each well.</span></li>
  <li><input type="checkbox"><span>Remove any residual liquid from the top of the plate. Seal firmly with the sealing film provided. A mechanical plate sealer gives the most consistent seal.</span></li>
  <li><input type="checkbox"><span>Place silicone compression mat on top of the sealing film. Sandwich the plate and mat between 2 Adapter Plate Sets. Shake at <strong>25 Hz for 5 min</strong>.</span></li>
  <li><input type="checkbox"><span>Re-orient the plate so the side closest to the machine body is now furthest. Shake again at <strong>25 Hz for 5 min</strong>. Do not exceed 2 × 5 min at 25 Hz.</span></li>
  <li><input type="checkbox"><span>Centrifuge the PowerBead Pro Plate at room temperature for <strong>6 min at 4500 × g</strong>.</span></li>
  <li><input type="checkbox"><span>Discard the sealing film. Transfer supernatant to collection microtubes (CMTRs). Expect 500–600 µL; some soil particles may remain.</span></li>
</ul>

---

## Part B: Inhibitor Removal

*~20–25 min*

<ul class="checklist">
  <li><input type="checkbox"><span>Add <strong>200 µL Solution CD2</strong> to each CMTR. For very high-inhibitor samples, use 250 µL. Seal with caps provided and vortex.</span></li>
  <li><input type="checkbox"><span>Centrifuge at room temperature for <strong>6 min at 4500 × g</strong>.</span></li>
  <li><input type="checkbox"><span>Transfer up to <strong>700 µL supernatant</strong> to an S-Block. Expect 500–600 µL.</span></li>
  <li><input type="checkbox"><span>Add <strong>600 µL Solution CD3</strong> to each well of the S-Block. Pipette up and down to mix. Place a QIAamp 96 Spin Plate onto a new S-Block.</span></li>
</ul>

---

## Part C: Binding & Washing

*~60–80 min*

<ul class="checklist">
  <li><input type="checkbox"><span>Load ~650 µL into each well of the spin plate. Seal with sealing tape. Centrifuge at room temperature for <strong>3 min at 4500 × g</strong>. Discard flow-through. Remove sealing tape.</span></li>
  <li><input type="checkbox"><span>Repeat loading and centrifugation until all supernatant has been processed. Discard final flow-through. Place spin plate back on the same S-Block.</span></li>
  <li><input type="checkbox"><span>Add <strong>500 µL Solution EA</strong> to each well. Seal with sealing tape. Centrifuge at room temperature for <strong>3 min at 4500 × g</strong>. Discard flow-through. Place spin plate back on S-Block.</span></li>
  <li><input type="checkbox"><span>Add <strong>500 µL Solution C5</strong> to the spin plate. Seal with sealing tape. Centrifuge for <strong>3 min at 4500 × g</strong>.</span></li>
  <li><input type="checkbox"><span>Discard flow-through (remove any trace from S-Block). Place spin plate (still sealed) back on the same S-Block. Centrifuge again at room temperature for <strong>5 min at 4500 × g</strong>. Discard flow-through.</span></li>
</ul>

---

## Part D: Elution

*~20 min*

<ul class="checklist">
  <li><input type="checkbox"><span>Carefully place the spin plate onto <strong>new collection microtubes</strong>. Discard sealing tape. Allow to air dry for <strong>10 min</strong> at room temperature.</span></li>
  <li><input type="checkbox"><span>Add <strong>100 µL Solution C6</strong> to the center of each well. Seal with sealing tape. Centrifuge at room temperature for <strong>3 min at 4500 × g</strong>. Discard sealing tape.</span></li>
  <li><input type="checkbox"><span>Seal the collection microtubes with caps provided. DNA is ready for downstream applications.</span></li>
</ul>

!!! tip "Storage"
    Store eluted DNA at −20°C for long-term use. Avoid repeated freeze-thaw cycles.

## Stop Points

!!! danger "Stop and ask before:"
    - Proceeding if the sealing film leaked during bead beating.
    - Using eluted DNA if A260/A280 or A260/A230 ratios are outside expected range (A260/A280 ~1.8, A260/A230 >1.5).
    - Skipping the 5 min air-dry step — residual ethanol from C5 will inhibit downstream reactions.
